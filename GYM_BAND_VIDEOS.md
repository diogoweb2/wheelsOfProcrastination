# Band exercises to review — two basement poles

Five candidates. **Nothing is in the catalog yet** — this is the review list.
Watch the short clip first; the long one only if you want the coaching.
Every link below was checked: it plays, and (except where noted) it can be
embedded in the app.

---

## 1. Band-resisted acceleration 🏃

Band round your waist, anchored to a pole **behind** you. Drive out 3–4 hard steps.

- ▶️ [31s — Live Lean TV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kW3mLx54b5M) — uses a partner holding the band; your pole replaces the partner
- ▶️ [2:56 — Simple Speed Coach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0XFX4xQFk4) — the how and why

**Why it's new:** horizontal power. Nothing in the catalog trains the pickleball first step.
**Needs:** one band + one pole. Have it today.

---

## 2. Band-resisted deceleration 🛑

Same setup as #1, reversed — the band pulls you out, you brake and back-pedal.

- ▶️ [37s — Evolve Flagstaff](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I1N2q744N20) — accel + decel in one clip
- ▶️ [2:02 — Athletic Over 40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObtMVH-7co4) — ⚠️ **cannot be embedded in the app**; if you pick this exercise the app gets the 37s clip instead

**Why it's new:** braking is where pickleball injuries live, and nothing here trains it.
**Needs:** one band + one pole. Same setup as #1 — one decision, two exercises.

---

## 3. Band pull-through 🍑

Low anchor **behind** you, band passes between your legs, hinge and stand up.

- ▶️ [45s — Power Plant Gym](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UBYx7d0VcCg)
- ▶️ [49s — Vintage CrossFit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NO-NevrDrjE)

**Why it's new:** a hip hinge with **zero spinal load**. Current hinges are the DB
Romanian deadlift and the kettlebell swing, and both spend your lower back.
**Needs:** one band + one pole. Have it today.

---

## 4. Lateral band walk ↔️

Band at ankles or hips, step sideways under tension.

- ▶️ [11s — Tom Pfeiffer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N28Hpdezg7Q)
- ▶️ [1:50 — GPS Human Performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wUk8wQNUT8) — why it hits the glute medius

**Why it's new:** hip abductors against horizontal resistance. Gravity can't supply
it, and it is the pickleball lateral pattern.
**Needs:** one band. No pole at all.

---

## 5. Band belt squat 🦵

Band under both feet, over the hips (or a belt) — squat against it.

- ▶️ [38s — Asphodel Fitness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=27dR_S7v-Vs)
- ▶️ [48s — Testosterone Nation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LabcPf9kIoE) — the anchored variation

**You're right: this one does not need the poles.** The band goes under your own
feet, so it is symmetric on its own — no anchor, no second band. The anchored
version (second link) is the one that would use a pole.

**Caveat before you pick it:** band tension is highest standing tall and slackest
at the bottom of the squat — backwards from where a squat is hard. It loads the
pattern without loading your spine, but it will never replace a heavy goblet squat.

**Needs:** one band. Have it today.

---

## Purchase decision: buy nothing

Earlier recommendation was a second heavy loop for the belt squat. That was wrong —
the belt squat loops under your feet. **All five of these work with the bands already
hanging in the basement.**
