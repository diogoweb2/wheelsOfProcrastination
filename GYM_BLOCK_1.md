# Block 1 — the six sessions

> Strength and pickleball durability — lower back and core first.

**You do the next one.** That is the whole scheduling rule — no Monday/Wednesday/Friday. Train twice this week and you get through S1 and S2; next week picks up at S3 and carries on. Train five times and you get through five. The rotation is never restarted by the calendar, only advanced by finishing a session.

Run it for **24 finished sessions** (four full trips round the rotation), and up to 42 if it is still progressing — measured in **sessions done, not weeks owned**, because eight weeks is 16 sessions at twice a week and 40 at five, and only one of those is a block's worth of training. The app counts for you and says when.

Then **Block 2 changes 2–4 movements and nothing else** — split squat → reverse lunge, flat press → incline, chest-supported row → one-arm, split squat jump → another lateral or vertical power move. The patterns stay. You want progressive exposure, not novelty.

| | Session | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** | 🦵 Lower strength + core | Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat · Bench Hip Thrust · Side Plank · Calf Raise · Back Extension |
| **S2** | 🫸 Upper push + pull | Dumbbell Bench Press · Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row · Pull-ups · Dips · Dumbbell Lateral Raise · Band Face Pull |
| **S3** | ⚡ Pickleball power + stability | Split Squat Jump · Lateral Shuffle · Kettlebell Swing · Copenhagen Plank · Band Pallof Press |
| **S4** | 🦿 Lower unilateral + posterior chain | Dumbbell Reverse Lunge · Goblet Squat · Single-Leg Glute Bridge · Band Leg Curl · Single-Leg Calf Raise |
| **S5** | 🧗 Upper pull + shoulder health | Pull-ups · One-Arm Dumbbell Row · Dumbbell Shoulder Press · Band External Rotation · Dumbbell Reverse Wrist Curl · Dumbbell Wrist Curl |
| **S6** | 🏓 Full body + pickleball | Dumbbell Step-up · Incline Dumbbell Bench Press · Band Lat Pulldown · Band Rotational Press · Farmer's Carry · Medicine Ball Chest Pass |

## How to read the numbers

**`3 × 8–12` is a range, not a target.** The low end is what has to be there for the set to count; the top is what you chase. Hit the top of the range on **every** set and the weight goes up next time — that is the entire progression rule, and the app applies it from what you actually log.

**⚡ means stop on quality, not on the count.** Jumps, shuffles and throws end the moment height, speed or landing goes, whatever the reps say. Chasing a number on those trains the wrong thing.

Weights are deliberately not written down here: the app carries what you lifted last time for each exercise and puts it in front of you.

## 20 · 30 · 40 minutes

The tables below are the **30-minute** session — what each one is written for. The other two lengths change how much of it you do, never which session it is:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **20 min** | exercises come off the **back** of the list, never below three | the accessories go; the main lift never does |
| **30 min** | exactly as written below | — |
| **40 min** | one extra set on each of the first two movements, and take the longer rests | more time buys more of what already matters, not more exercises. A ⚡ slot never gains a set, so S3 at 40 is the same as S3 at 30 — which is correct for a power session |

The order within a session is the priority order: main strength movement (3 sets) → second major movement (3) → the goal-specific one (2–3) → accessories and core (2). Five exercises is not a target; the sessions run four to six.

## S1 · 🦵 Lower strength + core

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🇧🇬 **Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat** | 3 × 8–12 /side | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Flat weight bench |
| 2 | 🍑 **Bench Hip Thrust** | 3 × 10–15 | Flat weight bench + Adjustable dumbbells on stand |
| 3 | 📏 **Side Plank** | 2 × 30–45s /side | Bodyweight |
| 4 | 🦶 **Calf Raise** | 3 × 15–20 | Bodyweight |
| 5 | 🐍 **Back Extension** | 2 × 10–15 | Hyperextension bench |

<details><summary>How to do them</summary>

**🇧🇬 Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat** — Rear foot on the bench, dumbbells at your sides, drop the back knee straight down and drive up through the front heel. Keep the front shin near vertical and the torso tall.

**🍑 Bench Hip Thrust** — Upper back on the bench, dumbbell across the hips, drive hips up to a straight line. Tuck the chin and squeeze glutes at the top, no back arch.

**📏 Side Plank** — On one forearm, hips stacked and lifted, body in a straight line. Both sides.

**🦶 Calf Raise** — Up on the toes as high as you can, slow all the way back down.

**🐍 Back Extension** — Hips on the pad, ankles under the rollers, arms crossed on the chest. Hinge down until you feel the hamstrings, then squeeze the glutes to come back to one straight line — stop level, never arch past it. Slow both ways; this is endurance work, not a lift.

</details>

## S2 · 🫸 Upper push + pull

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🏋️ **Dumbbell Bench Press** | 3 × 8–12 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Flat weight bench |
| 2 | 🛶 **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 3 × 8–12 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Adjustable Weight Bench |
| 3 | 🧗 **Pull-ups** | 3 × 4–8 | Pull-up bar |
| 4 | 🟨 **Dips** | 3 × 6–10 | Parallel dip bars |
| 5 | 🕊️ **Dumbbell Lateral Raise** | 2 × 15–20 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand |
| 6 | 😮 **Band Face Pull** | 2 × 15–20 | Resistance bands |

- **Pull-ups** — Chin-ups instead are fine — same slot, easier line of pull.
- **Dips** — Stop at parallel. Deeper than that is the shoulder paying for the rep.

<details><summary>How to do them</summary>

**🏋️ Dumbbell Bench Press** — Lie flat, dumbbells at chest level, press up until arms are straight over your chest. Keep both feet planted and don't arch the lower back off the bench.

**🛶 Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** — Set the backrest to about 45 degrees and lie face-down on it, row both dumbbells to your ribs. Chest stays glued to the pad the whole set.

**🧗 Pull-ups** — Hang, pull until your chin clears the bar, lower all the way down under control.

**🟨 Dips** — Support yourself on the bars, lower until your upper arms are about parallel to the floor, press back up. Stop at parallel — going deeper strains the shoulder.

**🕊️ Dumbbell Lateral Raise** — Stand tall, light dumbbells at your sides, raise them out to shoulder height with a slight elbow bend. Lead with the elbows, stop at shoulder level, lower slowly.

**😮 Band Face Pull** — Anchor slightly above head height. Pull the band towards your forehead, hands splitting apart and elbows high, ending with the knuckles beside the ears. Pause a beat, return slowly. Rear delts and upper back — keep it light.

</details>

## S3 · ⚡ Pickleball power + stability

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ✂️ **Split Squat Jump** ⚡ | 3 × 5–8 | Bodyweight |
| 2 | ↔️ **Lateral Shuffle** ⚡ | 4 × 15–20 | Bodyweight |
| 3 | 🔔 **Kettlebell Swing** ⚡ | 3 × 8–12 | Kettlebell |
| 4 | 🇩🇰 **Copenhagen Plank** | 2 × 20–30s /side | Flat weight bench |
| 5 | 🛡️ **Band Pallof Press** | 3 × 8–12 /side | Resistance bands |

- **Split Squat Jump** — Stop the set the moment height or landing quality drops.
- **Lateral Shuffle** — Short, sharp efforts. This is not a cardio circuit.
- **Kettlebell Swing** — Explosive, not a rep count. Full rest between sets, and stop the moment the hinge gets sloppy.

<details><summary>How to do them</summary>

**✂️ Split Squat Jump** — From a split stance, jump and switch legs in the air, landing softly. Three per side. Stop the set the moment jump height, landing control or speed drops — even at two reps. Full rest between sets; never take this near failure.

**↔️ Lateral Shuffle** — Stay low in a half squat and shuffle sideways a few steps each way. Keep the chest up and feet from crossing.

**🔔 Kettlebell Swing** — Hinge and hike the bell back, then snap the hips to float it to chest height. Arms stay relaxed, the hips do the work.

**🇩🇰 Copenhagen Plank** — Side plank on your forearm with the top leg's inner knee or ankle resting on the bench, lift the hips. Start with the knee version; keep the body in one line.

**🛡️ Band Pallof Press** — Anchor at chest height on the post. Stand side-on with the band held at the sternum, press it straight out and hold 2 s while the band tries to rotate you, then bring it back in. Resist the twist — nothing above the hips should move.

</details>

## S4 · 🦿 Lower unilateral + posterior chain

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔙 **Dumbbell Reverse Lunge** | 3 × 8–12 /side | Adjustable dumbbells on stand |
| 2 | 🏺 **Goblet Squat** | 3 × 10–15 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand |
| 3 | 🦿 **Single-Leg Glute Bridge** | 3 × 10–15 /side | Bodyweight |
| 4 | 🦵 **Band Leg Curl** | 3 × 12–20 /side | Resistance bands |
| 5 | 🦶 **Single-Leg Calf Raise** | 3 × 15–20 /side | Adjustable dumbbells on stand |

<details><summary>How to do them</summary>

**🔙 Dumbbell Reverse Lunge** — Hold dumbbells at your sides, step back and drop the rear knee toward the floor, then drive forward to stand. Stepping backwards keeps the front knee calm.

**🏺 Goblet Squat** — Hold one dumbbell vertically at your chest, squat down between your knees and stand up. Elbows inside the knees, chest up, weight stays close to the body.

**🦿 Single-Leg Glute Bridge** — Lie on your back, one foot planted and the other leg straight up, push the hips up level. Keep the hips square — don't let one side drop.

**🦵 Band Leg Curl** — Anchor low. Lie face down, loop the band around one ankle, curl the heel towards the glutes against the pull, then lower slowly all the way. Keep the hips flat on the floor.

**🦶 Single-Leg Calf Raise** — Stand on one foot holding a dumbbell, rise onto the ball of the foot and lower slowly. Hold something for balance if needed.

</details>

## S5 · 🧗 Upper pull + shoulder health

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🧗 **Pull-ups** | 3 × 4–8 | Pull-up bar |
| 2 | 🚣 **One-Arm Dumbbell Row** | 3 × 8–12 /side | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Flat weight bench |
| 3 | 🎪 **Dumbbell Shoulder Press** | 3 × 8–12 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Adjustable Weight Bench |
| 4 | 🩹 **Band External Rotation** | 2 × 12–20 /side | Resistance bands |
| 5 | ✋ **Dumbbell Reverse Wrist Curl** | 2 × 15–20 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Flat weight bench |
| 6 | 🤲 **Dumbbell Wrist Curl** | 2 × 15–20 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Flat weight bench |

- **Pull-ups** — Chin-ups instead are fine — same slot.

<details><summary>How to do them</summary>

**🧗 Pull-ups** — Hang, pull until your chin clears the bar, lower all the way down under control.

**🚣 One-Arm Dumbbell Row** — One hand and same-side knee on the bench, row the dumbbell to your hip. Keep the back flat and square — no twisting the torso to lift it.

**🎪 Dumbbell Shoulder Press** — Backrest fully upright, sit with your back flat against it, press dumbbells from shoulder height to overhead. Keep the back on the pad — no leaning back to push.

**🩹 Band External Rotation** — Anchor at elbow height. Stand side-on, working arm nearest the anchor tucked at 90° with the elbow pinned to the ribs, rotate the forearm away from the body, then back with control. Light tension only — this is cuff work.

**✋ Dumbbell Reverse Wrist Curl** — Same seated position but palms down, wrists off the knee or bench edge. Lift the back of the hand up, go light, control the way down.

**🤲 Dumbbell Wrist Curl** — Sit, rest forearms on your thighs or the bench with palms up and wrists past the edge. Curl the weight up with the hand only, no forearm movement.

</details>

## S6 · 🏓 Full body + pickleball

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🪜 **Dumbbell Step-up** | 3 × 8–12 /side | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Flat weight bench |
| 2 | 📈 **Incline Dumbbell Bench Press** | 3 × 8–12 | Adjustable dumbbells on stand + Adjustable Weight Bench |
| 3 | ⬇️ **Band Lat Pulldown** | 3 × 10–15 | Resistance bands |
| 4 | 🌀 **Band Rotational Press** | 3 × 8–12 /side | Resistance bands |
| 5 | 🚜 **Farmer's Carry** | 3 × 30–45s | Adjustable dumbbells on stand |
| 6 | 🎯 **Medicine Ball Chest Pass** ⚡ | 3 × 4–6 | Medicine ball |

- **Medicine Ball Chest Pass** — Explosive throws. Reset between reps.

<details><summary>How to do them</summary>

**🪜 Dumbbell Step-up** — Dumbbells at your sides, step one foot onto the bench and stand up fully without pushing off the back leg. Lower under control, don't drop down.

**📈 Incline Dumbbell Bench Press** — Set the backrest to 30-45 degrees, press the dumbbells from upper-chest level to straight overhead. Shoulder blades stay pinned back against the pad.

**⬇️ Band Lat Pulldown** — Anchor high — door top or the post above head height. Kneel or sit facing the anchor, arms straight overhead, pull the handles down to the chest leading with the elbows. Let the shoulder blades travel down, then return all the way up.

**🌀 Band Rotational Press** — Anchor at chest height. Stand side-on, band in both hands at the chest, then rotate through the back hip and press away explosively — the pickleball drive. Return under control and reset. Speed, not grind.

**🚜 Farmer's Carry** — A dumbbell in each hand, walk tall with the shoulders back and short quick steps. Stop when your posture breaks, not when your grip does.

**🎯 Medicine Ball Chest Pass** — Stand facing a wall, ball at the chest. Drive it into the wall as hard as you can and catch the rebound. Stop the set the moment the throw slows — even at two reps. Full rest between sets.

</details>
