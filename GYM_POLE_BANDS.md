# Pole-anchored band work — the basement posts

Everything here anchors a band to **one basement pole**, at whatever height the
movement wants. The two places where the **second pole** actually earns its keep
are marked 🅿️🅿️.

**Nothing here is in the catalog yet** unless the row says *already in the app*.
Watch the short clip first; the long one only if you want the coaching.
Every link plays and every link can be embedded in the app.

---

# 🔻 Anchor at the FLOOR (base of the pole)

## 1. Band pull-through 🍑
Band passes between your legs from behind, hinge at the hips and stand tall.

- ▶️ [45s — Power Plant Gym](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UBYx7d0VcCg)
- ▶️ [49s — Vintage CrossFit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NO-NevrDrjE)

**Why it's worth adding:** a hip hinge with **zero spinal load**. Your only hinges
now are the DB Romanian deadlift and the kettlebell swing — both spend your lower back.

## 2. Band knee drive (sprinter hip flexion) 🦵
Band round one ankle, anchored low behind you. Drive the knee up hard, control it down.

- ▶️ [8s — Flex Affect](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KCRG5rDiJFQ) — the shape in one loop
- ▶️ [1:55 — DHolt Strength](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZBk88fEfYQ) — the how-to

**Why it's worth adding:** hip flexors under load, which nothing in the catalog
touches, and it is the muscle that produces the first step to a drop shot.

## 3. Standing hip adduction / abduction ↔️
Band round one ankle, anchored low to the side. Sweep the leg across the body
(adduction) or away from it (abduction).

- ▶️ [43s — Live Lean TV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugWMLte1FiM) — **adduction**
- ▶️ [1:23 — PersonalBestOz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cvXYrZpsy6k) — **abduction**

**Why it's worth adding:** groin and glute medius against **horizontal** resistance.
Your Copenhagen plank holds the adductors still; this one makes them do work through
a range. Pickleball tears groins.

# 🔶 Anchor at WAIST height (belt or loop round the hips)

## 4. Band-resisted acceleration 🏃
Anchored **behind** you. Drive out 3–4 hard steps against the pull.

- ▶️ [31s — Live Lean TV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kW3mLx54b5M) — uses a partner; your pole replaces them
- ▶️ [2:56 — Simple Speed Coach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0XFX4xQFk4)

## 5. Band-resisted deceleration 🛑
Same rig, reversed — the band pulls you out, you brake and back-pedal under control.

- ▶️ [37s — Evolve Flagstaff](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I1N2q744N20) — accel + decel in one clip

## 6. Band-resisted lateral shuffle ↔️
Anchored to the side. Stay low, shuffle away from the pole, shuffle back.

- ▶️ [12s — PeakForceAthletics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3q5yZbNm5o)
- ▶️ [40s — Kinetic Sports PT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WiKjiJfnmoI)

## 7. Band-resisted lateral bound (skater) ⛸️
Anchored to the side. Explode sideways off one leg, land on the other and stick it.

- ▶️ [18s — Miguel Aragoncillo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aMh6dytv8g8)
- ▶️ [28s — Champion PT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H1TXiC15RjY)

**Why #4–7 are the real prize:** all four are **horizontal power**, which gravity
cannot supply and no dumbbell can load. The catalog's power block is jumps and a
swing — all vertical. This is the pickleball block it is missing.

# 🔺 Anchor ABOVE your head (high on the pole)

## 10. Half-kneeling high-to-low chop 🪓
Band anchored high, half-kneeling, pull it down and across the body.

- ▶️ [35s — Eric Cressey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYVKVAbbFTM) — this is the coach you want on rotation
- ▶️ [57s — Resolute S&C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoAJy1_zYB0)

**Why it's worth adding:** trunk rotation under load in the diagonal a forehand
actually travels. The rotational press covers the push; this covers the pull.

# 🅿️🅿️ What the SECOND pole buys you

Not new movements — **both directions without re-rigging.** That is the whole gain,
and it is bigger than it sounds, because re-anchoring mid-set is where you stop.

- **A shuffle corridor.** One band per pole, both clipped to your belt. Shuffle
  left and the right band resists; shuffle right and the left one does. #6 becomes
  continuous instead of shuffle-out-shuffle-back.
- **Accel + decel in one set.** Band behind you resists the drive out, band in
  front resists the back-pedal. #4 and #5 become one drill.
- **A dual-anchor anti-rotation hold.** A band from each pole at chest height,
  pulling you opposite ways, and you stand still. Harder than a Pallof press
  because there is no side to lean into. ⚠️ No good clip exists for this — it's
  a Pallof press with a second band, so watch #8 and add the second band.

# Left out on purpose

Your gear notes say bands are never used where a dumbbell does the job, so these
did **not** make the list: band chest press, band fly, band row, band lat pulldown,
band curl, band pushdown, band wrist work, band face pull, band glute kickback.
The dumbbells, the bench and the pull-up bar already cover every one of them.
